No target was named. Stage 0 · pathway-expert

Three candidate targets, ranked by the evidence under them

The prompt named a therapeutic area, not a protein. The pathway stage does not answer with one target: it returns tiered candidates, each with the evidence it rests on and the reason it might fail. The tiers are a closed set, so "this is a guess" cannot be quietly promoted to "this is validated" by confident prose.

It also checks its own references. Of 8 DOIs this stage cited, 7 resolved to papers in the corpus and one did not — 10.1016/isci.2025.112047 — and the report says so rather than dropping it. Every later stage is checked the same way (5/5 at the literature stage, 2/2 at the structure stage). A citation a reader cannot follow is worse than no citation.

Stage 1 · molecular-biology-expert

Is there evidence behind the interface, or only a picture of it?

The winning candidate is a Class B GPCR heterodimer: CALCRL only becomes a high-affinity CGRP receptor when it partners RAMP1, and that obligate pairing is what makes the interface worth disrupting. The stage came back GO on grounds it stated: High-resolution human cryo-EM structure (PDB 6E3Y) and literature-validated extracellular hotspots (RAMP1 F83, W84, P85) provide a clinically validated target for competitive disruption.

It also fixed the affinity to beat and named prior hotspots to aim at — F83, W84, P85 on RAMP1 — before any structure was analysed, so the structural stage had a published expectation to be checked against rather than a blank sheet.

Which part of a membrane protein a binder can actually reach is decided here, not left to chance. The site hint carried forward reads: Extracellular domain CGRP-contacting epitope from cryo-EM structure doi:10.1038/s41586-018-0535-y and crystal structure doi:10.1016/j.molcel.2015.04.018; transmembrane residues (CALCRL W254/Y255/H295) explicitly excluded

That exclusion matters more than it sounds. In an isolated structure a transmembrane helix is an exposed hydrophobic slab, and it preferentially attracts binders — ones that cannot work in a cell, where that surface is buried in lipid. Reachable and clinically proven is the extracellular domain: erenumab blocks this receptor for migraine that way.

Between stages · deterministic

Choosing a structure is a measurement, not a preference

Best-evidenced and best-designable are different questions, and the literature answers the first one. It cites the landmark paper — for a receptor, the full-length agonist-bound cryo-EM complex. That is the right structure to understand the biology and the wrong one to hand a binder designer. The choice is made before the LLM sees anything, by comparing the entries themselves:

PDB 6E3Y

490 aa

target chain · 3.3 Å · 7 entities · 4 scaffolding chains

What the corpus cites: the full-length agonist-bound cryo-EM complex, with a nanobody, a G protein and the CGRP peptide in the way.

PDB 3N7S

115 aa

target chain · 2.1 Å · 2 entities · 0 scaffolding chains

What the pipeline designed against: the ectodomain complex alone, at higher resolution and a quarter the size.

Recorded verbatim as the run's own checkpoint: 6E3Y carries 4 scaffolding chain(s) (nanobody / G protein / fusion partner) against 0 in 3N7S, at no worse resolution (2.1 A vs 3.3 A). It fires only on a measurable defect — partner absent, target chain a fusion construct, or extra scaffolding chains at no better resolution — and --pdb always overrides it. Designing against 6E3Y would have meant a 490-residue target dominated by a nanobody, a G protein and the agonist peptide; 3N7S is the same interface with nothing else in the way.

Stage 2 · complex-structure-analysis

A membrane target, reduced to the face a binder can reach

The CGRP receptor is a class B GPCR heterodimer, and most of it is unreachable. The transmembrane bundle sits in lipid; in an isolated structure that surface is an exposed hydrophobic slab which preferentially attracts binders that cannot work in a cell. The extracellular domains are what antibodies actually hit — erenumab blocks this receptor for migraine.

Chain D was confirmed as the target by aligning its modelled residues against the UniProt sequence, not by trusting an upstream stage's chain letter — a swap yields real, correctly-numbered residues on the wrong protein, which hotspot checking alone cannot catch. The interface buries 2,385 Å².

10 hotspots were selected, against a cap of 12. More is not stricter: RFD3's hit rate falls as the set grows, which weakens the engagement gate rather than tightening it.

Trp74, Phe83, Trp84, Pro85, Asp90, Phe93, Leu94, His97, Phe101, Ile106

The trim kept 84 residues in 1 contiguous segment. A cut is refused outright if it would open hydrophobic core near the epitope — a fresh hydrophobic face is precisely what a diffusion model binds by preference, so an over-eager trim manufactures its own false positives.

Stage 5 · calibration

Measure the hit rate before buying the GPU time

The pipeline never scales straight to production. It folds a trial — 527 backbones, 2,108 refolds — counts how many clear the success bar, and extrapolates with a Wilson interval rather than a point estimate. Here 99/527 backbones produced an excellent design: 18.79%, 95% CI 15.68–22.34%.

sized onbackbonesrefolds GPU-hdisk
point estimate293 1,065 2.8 2.7 GB
Wilson 95% lower bound351 1,275 3.4 3.2 GB

The bar went up, not down. A trial this good changes what is worth asking for: the run was requested at iptm > 0.7 and sized at iptm > 0.85 — the strictest rung that still clears five hits and still fits the budget. It only ever raises. The verdict — SCALE_UP — is a manifest checkpoint either way, so a campaign that should not run stops here, and the plan is persisted: resuming with --start-from production in a fresh process re-derives this sizing instead of falling back to the config default.

The gates are not optional, and the two doing the most work are the two a confidence score cannot replace. RF3 cannot be given a docked pose at inference, so ipTM reports confidence in whatever interface the model chose for itself — ranking on it alone selects confidently mis-docked binders. binder_rmsd_dock is the gate that catches them.
