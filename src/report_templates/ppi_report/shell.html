Read this before acting on anything below

Every design in this report is an unvalidated computational hypothesis — a sequence and a predicted pose. Nothing here has been expressed, purified or assayed, and no number on this page is a measurement of binding. The gates and rankings compare predictions against each other; they cannot tell you that a design binds.

If you synthesise anything derived from this report, screening the sequence is your responsibility — use a synthesis provider that screens orders. See docs/responsible-use.md in the repository for intended use, what the confidence metrics do and do not tell you, and this project's position on safety classifiers.
