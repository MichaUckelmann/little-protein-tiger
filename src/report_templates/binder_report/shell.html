Do the winners actually bind where intended?

ipTM and ipSAE say the model is confident. They don't say the binder is sitting on the intended epitope rather than confidently docked somewhere else. Two more measurements close that gap.

Hotspot engagement — the fraction of the intended hotspot residues actually contacted by the refolded binder (all-atom, 8 Å) — and epitope recall — how much of the binder's contact footprint overlaps the target-intel stage's intended epitope — are the two checks that catch a confidently mis-docked design that ipTM alone would miss.
